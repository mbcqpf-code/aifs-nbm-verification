Apples-to-Apples Synoptic Hourly Evaluation vs. NOAA URMA Analysis

ℹ️ Apples-to-Apples Verification Methodology:

Because the AIFS Open Data ensemble delivers 6-hourly outputs, comparing it against continuous 24-hour daily extremes creates artificial bias. To ensure an objective evaluation, AIFS, NBM, and URMA are sampled at the exact same hourly timestamps:

  • Minimum Temperature Proxy: Evaluated directly at 12:00 UTC across AIFS, NBM, and URMA.
  • Maximum Temperature Proxy: Evaluated as $\max(18\text{z}, 00\text{z})$ across AIFS, NBM, and URMA.
  • Cycles: 00z AIFS Ensemble Mean vs. 06z NBM Core (13z operational package) on a shared 0.25° CONUS mesh.
